Important Information
💰 Pricing & VAT

All prices shown exclude VAT. VAT will apply once Ember Automations is VAT registered. Monthly, 6-month, and 12-month contract terms are available — discounts apply for longer commitments.

🔒 Slot Limits Are Real

WhatsApp Community daily slots are limited to 2 per day across all Network and Dominant clients. Homepage leaderboard slots are capped at 4. Once full, new clients are waitlisted. Act early.

💬 WhatsApp API Note

The WhatsApp Chatbot (directory) runs on the Meta Business API. Approval takes 2–4 weeks. This is handled by Ember Automations on your behalf — no additional cost to you.

🏆 Category Exclusivity

Dominant tier category exclusivity means no competitor appears in your sector in the WhatsApp chatbot. Pricing varies by category size and competition. Contact us for a tailored quote.

📅 Contracts & Cancellation

A 30-day written notice is required to cancel or downgrade. Dominant clients receive 60 days' first-right-of-renewal notice before their slot is offered to any other advertiser.

📊 Reports & Transparency

All paid tiers from Local upward receive performance data. Network and Dominant clients receive a full automated PDF report monthly covering impressions, clicks, WhatsApp engagement, and social reach.
