A dynamic, modern Bento Box style news hub with vibrant colors, glassmorphism, and highly interactive cards.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop-first
- Theme: Light, vibrant, dynamic, modern layout
- Background: Cool Light Gray (#F8FAFC)
- Surface: White (#FFFFFF) and Frosted Glass (rgba(255,255,255,0.7)) for cards
- Primary Accent: Electric Blue (#3B82F6) or Neon Purple (#8B5CF6) for the "Subscribe" button and active states
- Text Primary: Charcoal (#0F172A)
- Text Secondary: Steel Gray (#475569)
- Cards: Generously rounded corners (16px and 24px), varied sizes in a Bento Box grid, soft layered drop shadows for elevation.
- Ads: Vibrant, interactive sponsor modules that fit perfectly into the varied tile sizes of the grid.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header (Preserved):** A floating, pill-shaped navigation bar (rounded-full). Contains logo "Bushbuck**News**" on left, links "Local", "Sports", "Business" centered, and an electric blue "Subscribe" button on the right. Frosted glass effect (backdrop-blur-xl) on scroll.
2. **Featured Mosaic (Hero):** A dynamic Bento Box grid. The largest tile is the top story. Smaller tiles around it show trending news.
3. **Interactive Sidebar Ad:** A sticky, premium vertical ad space on the right side of the layout that scrolls beautifully.
4. **Categories Carousel:** A laterally scrolling row of pill-shaped category tags with smooth micro-animations on hover.
5. **Multimedia Section:** A visually rich area for video news or photo galleries, with dark overlays and play buttons.
6. **Native Sponsor Tile:** A 2x2 grid tile styled specifically for a featured brand, seamlessly matching the UI but visually distinct as an ad.
7. **Footer:** Playful but structured, with large typography and bold social links.
