A sleek, high-contrast dark mode news platform with neon accents, tech-forward aesthetic, and cinematic ad placements.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop-first
- Theme: Dark, sleek, high-contrast, tech-inspired
- Background: Deep Obsidian (#09090B)
- Surface: Matte Dark Gray (#18181B) with subtle 1px inner borders (#27272A)
- Primary Accent: Brilliant Cyan (#06B6D4) or Electric Lime (#84CC16) for the "Subscribe" button and live indicators
- Text Primary: Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- Text Secondary: Cool Gray (#A1A1AA)
- Cards: Subtly rounded (8px), no shadow but subtle glowing borders on hover.
- Ads: Cinematic ad placements designed for rich media or embedded video, framed with sleek, dark borders.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header (Preserved):** A floating, pill-shaped navigation bar (rounded-full) with dark frosted glass (bg-black/60). Contains logo "Bushbuck**News**" on left, links "Local", "Sports", "Business" centered, and an electric cyan "Subscribe" button on the right.
2. **Live News Ticker:** A discreet horizontally sliding marquee immediately below the header for breaking news.
3. **Cinematic Hero:** A massive edge-to-edge dark image with text overlay on a subtle gradient, focusing on the main headline.
4. **Immersive Ad Takeover:** A large, parallax-style full-width ad section that reveals smoothly as the user scrolls.
5. **Trending Feed:** A dense, highly readable vertical list of articles with thumbnail images on the right side. Hovering over a row highlights the text in cyan.
6. **Sponsor 'Quick Bites':** Small, highly interactive ad slots placed strategically between every 4-5 news items in the list.
7. **Footer:** Minimalist grid layout, stark contrast, and bold subscribe section.
