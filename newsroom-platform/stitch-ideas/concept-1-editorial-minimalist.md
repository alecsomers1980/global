A clean, high-end editorial news platform with generous whitespace, sharp typography, and native-feeling ad placements.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop-first
- Theme: Light, sophisticated, minimal editorial
- Background: Warm Off-White (#FBFAF8)
- Surface: Crisp White (#FFFFFF) for article cards
- Primary Accent: Editorial Red (#D32F2F) for the "Subscribe" button, "News" logo text, and breaking tags
- Text Primary: Deep Ink Black (#1A1A1A) for headlines
- Text Secondary: Muted Slate (#64748B) for dates, authors, and excerpts
- Cards: Minimal borders (1px solid #E5E7EB), sharp corners (0px), subtle hover lift shadow
- Ads: Delineated with a soft gray background (#F3F4F6) and small "Sponsored" tag, avoiding harsh banners.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header (Preserved):** A floating, pill-shaped navigation bar (rounded-full). Contains logo "Bushbuck**News**" on left, links "Local", "Sports", "Business" centered, and a primary red "Subscribe" button on the right. Frosted glass effect (backdrop-blur-xl) on scroll.
2. **Hero Section:** A prominent, full-width featured article with a large high-quality image, bold serif headline, and publisher metadata.
3. **Top Ad Slot:** A sleek, horizontal banner area gracefully integrated below the hero section.
4. **Latest News Grid:** A 3-column masonry or grid layout of article cards. Each card has a hover effect (image slight zoom, title underline). 
5. **In-Feed Sponsor Block:** An interactive, visually distinct module within the grid for premium ad placement. Should look like a high-end brand partnership.
6. **Newsletter Section:** A wide, minimalist band prompting email signups for daily curated news.
7. **Footer:** Clean layout with category links, social links, and copyright.
