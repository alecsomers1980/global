1  The website at a glance

Your new site is a modern, fast, search-optimised platform built to serve two audiences at once:
- Employers who need staff — permanent, contract or temporary (TES).
- Job seekers who want work, plus paid tools and services to help them get hired.
It is far more than a brochure site. It already includes a job portal, an online shop, a paid e-book, an affiliate programme, a customer service portal, an AI article engine, and a full admin back office — all under one roof, built to rank on Google and to be quoted by AI search tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.
Foundations: a modern framework (Next.js) with a Supabase database, PayFast payments, structured data for search engines, automatic sitemaps, an llms.txt file for AI crawlers, security hardening, and a polished parallax visual design.
2  Public pages — what each one does

Accounts & self-service areas
- Login / Sign-up / Forgot password — secure accounts with email verification.
- Affiliate Dashboard — affiliates see their referral link, tracked sales, commission earned, and can enter banking details for payouts.
- Customer Order Portal — buyers of a done-for-you service get a private tracking link to upload documents, book a fingerprint appointment, see order status and turnaround, download the finished file, and request a revision — no login required.
3  The admin area — what you control

The admin back office (restricted to your team) is the control centre. You manage the catalogue, content, payouts and order flow yourselves — no developer needed for day-to-day changes.
4  How the key features work

This section explains, in plain terms, how your money-making and content features actually operate day to day.
4.1  The Shop — digital products & done-for-you services
The shop sells two kinds of products to job seekers:
- Instant downloads — CV templates (private and government sector) — paid for once and downloaded instantly.
- Done-for-you services — CV Revamp, Cover Letter writing, Criminal Record Checks, Qualification Verification and Umalusi Matric Certification — where a specialist completes the work for the customer.
How a purchase works:
1.  The customer browses the Shop and opens a product page.
2.  They check out and pay securely via PayFast (card or instant EFT).
3.  Instant products: they immediately receive a secure download link.
4.  Services: the order opens in your admin 'Service jobs' pipeline, and the customer gets a private tracking link — no login needed.
5.  Depending on the service, the customer uploads documents (e.g. their current CV for a Revamp) or books an in-person fingerprint appointment (Criminal Record Check) — with consent captured where the law requires it.
6.  Your team moves the job through Received → In progress → Delivered and uploads the finished file.
7.  The customer downloads the deliverable from their tracking link, and can request a revision if the service includes one.
You control:  products, prices, downloadable files, turnaround times and which products are visible — all under Admin → Shop and Admin → Service jobs.
4.2  The Affiliate Program — referral commissions
A free, self-running way for anyone to earn commission by promoting your job-hunting e-book — turning fans and partners into a sales force.
1.  Someone signs up to become an affiliate (with email verification and anti-spam protection).
2.  You review the application under Admin → Affiliates and approve it; the system issues a unique tracking code.
3.  The affiliate logs into their own dashboard and gets a unique referral link.
4.  They share that link on social media, a blog, WhatsApp or by word of mouth.
5.  When a visitor clicks the link, the site remembers that affiliate for 60 days.
6.  If that visitor buys the e-book, the sale is automatically credited to the affiliate and a commission is recorded.
7.  The affiliate sees their earnings (pending, approved, paid) in their dashboard and enters their banking details.
8.  You review commissions and mark them approved or paid under Admin → E-book.
You control:  who gets approved, the commission rate (a percentage or a fixed amount), and when commissions are paid out.
4.3  The Job-Hunting E-book
1.  You upload the e-book file and set its price and the affiliate commission under Admin → E-book.
2.  A customer buys it on the E-book page via secure PayFast checkout.
3.  They receive an instant download.
4.  If they arrived through an affiliate's link, that affiliate automatically earns commission.
You control:  the file, the price, the commission, and a view of every sale.
4.4  Insights — the AI article engine
Builds search authority and brings in traffic, while keeping you in full control of what actually goes live.
1.  Twice a month the system automatically writes two draft articles using AI, rotating through five topics: hiring, labour law, careers, employment trends, and payroll & HR.
2.  Drafts wait safely under Admin → Insights — nothing is published automatically without your approval.
3.  You open a draft, edit anything you like, then either Approve it (to publish on a chosen date) or Publish now.
4.  You can also Write a new article by hand, or Generate one with AI on demand, at any time.
5.  Published articles appear on the Insights page, formatted for Google and quotable by AI search tools such as ChatGPT.
You control:  every word — review, edit, approve, schedule, publish or discard each article.
4.5  Jobs & Submit CV
1.  The Jobs page displays your live vacancies straight from PlacementPartner, so it is always current with no manual updates.
2.  The Submit CV page lets job seekers send their CV directly into your PlacementPartner talent pool.
4.6  Customer & affiliate self-service
- Affiliate dashboard — affiliates manage their own link, see their sales and earnings, and capture their banking details.
- Customer order portal — a private, password-free link where service customers track status, upload documents, book appointments, download the finished work and request revisions.

6  Growth opportunities

A.  Competitor benchmark — what others do, and what we can add
Leading South African recruitment and labour-broking sites commonly offer the following. Where H&S already matches them it is noted; the rest are opportunities.
B.  Functionality nobody else is really doing
- AI CV review & score — a free tool where a job seeker uploads a CV and gets instant AI feedback (and an upsell to the CV Revamp service).
- AI job-matching — match candidates to live vacancies automatically and notify them.
- AI assistant / chatbot — answers job-seeker and employer questions 24/7, helps people apply, and books employer call-backs.
- Interactive compliance helper — a short quiz telling an employer their TES/LRA obligations, capturing the lead.
- Application tracker — job seekers see where their application stands — rare and very sticky.
- Deeper monetisation — bundle shop services, recurring screening subscriptions, and an expanded affiliate model.
C.  AI automation opportunities (specific to labour broking & recruitment)
Your services are recruitment, TES, payroll, vetting, HR/IR and CV handling. AI automation can take cost and turnaround time out of each:
1.  AI CV screening & ranking — automatically parse incoming CVs, match them to a job spec, and rank candidates — cutting shortlisting time dramatically (supports CV Response Handling).
2.  AI candidate–job matching & alerts — continuously match your talent pool to open roles and auto-notify the best fits.
3.  AI job-spec & advert writer — turn an employer brief into a polished job description and advert in seconds.
4.  Automated vetting workflows — orchestrate criminal, credit, qualification and Umalusi checks, with tracking and auto-reminders.
5.  WhatsApp & email automation — application confirmations, interview invites, document requests and placement updates, sent automatically.
6.  AI chatbot (dual) — one trained for job seekers, one for employers (request staff, qualify the lead, book a consult).
7.  Compliance automation — auto-generate TES/BCEA-compliant contracts and trigger reminders for renewals and statutory dates.
8.  Payroll & timesheet automation — OCR/auto-capture of timesheets, validation and payroll-ready exports.
9.  AI content engine (already live) — the Insights generator; extendable to LinkedIn posts, newsletters and case studies.
10.  Candidate re-engagement — automatically reactivate dormant candidates when a matching role appears.
11.  Lead scoring & follow-up — score employer enquiries and trigger the right follow-up sequence.
12.  AI interview prep — a paid or lead-gen tool offering mock interviews and feedback (ties into the e-book audience).
