A small once-off setup, then one simple monthly fee to host, maintain and grow your site.
June 2026  ·  Valid for 30 days


1  How it works

- Once-off setup — a small fee to configure, launch and hand over the site.
- Monthly plan — one predictable fee that covers hosting, security, maintenance, support, the AI Insights engine, and a bucket of update/improvement time each month.
- Grow as you go — larger new features from your roadmap are built progressively inside the higher plan, or quoted as separate mini-projects — so the site keeps growing with your business.
There are no separate hosting, database or AI-article invoices — those running costs are bundled into your monthly fee. One number, no surprises.
2  Once-off setup

Deliberately kept low: the full platform build is worth far more, but the setup is affordable because the plan recovers it over time.
3  Choose a monthly plan


Recommended: the Grow plan — enough to genuinely run and improve the site each month, while larger features remain easy, separate decisions.

From Year 2, it is simply R66,000 / year — a fraction of a large once-off build, with the site continually maintained and improved.
6  Plan terms

- Hosting, database and AI-article running costs are included — one monthly fee, no surprise invoices.
- Payment-gateway fees on your own sales (PayFast, ~2.9% + R2 per transaction) are billed by PayFast directly.
- Available month-to-month, or on a 12-month term at a small discount.
- Included monthly hours do not roll over; larger features are scoped and approved separately.
- You own your content, domain and data throughout.
