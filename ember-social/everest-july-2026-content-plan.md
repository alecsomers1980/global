# Everest Motoring — July 2026 Social Plan

**Brief in one line:** Make the feed feel premium and lifestyle-led — show *where the cars take you*, not the price box — while keeping the maintenance tips and car-feature posts, and adding two cinematic lifestyle video clips.

**Why July works:** In Mpumalanga, July is mid-winter dry season — peak Kruger game-viewing, the Panorama Route in golden light, and school-holiday road trips. The season does a lot of the premium work for us; the grade and headline restraint do the rest.

Runs through Ember Social's six-template system (`lifestyle_hero`, `studio`, `spec_card`, `multi_car`, `tip_card`, `sell_yours`), one branded AI image per post (yellow accent, logo top-left), all scenes set in real SA locations.

---

## 1. Look-and-Feel Direction (premium + lifestyle-led)

- **Lead with `lifestyle_hero`.** It becomes the spine of the feed. Vehicle is the hero *in a place* (Panorama Route viewpoint, Kruger gate, lowveld farm road), not isolated on a backdrop.
- **Colour grade = warm editorial.** Golden-hour highlights, deep controlled shadows, slightly desaturated mid-tones — Sony A7R V + film-stock LUT. Avoid the over-saturated "classifieds" look.
- **Let scenes breathe.** Wide negative space, low horizon, car small-to-medium in frame on landscape posts. Premium = room, not clutter.
- **Headline restraint.** On `studio`, hold the ALL-CAPS tagline to 2-3 words; one idea per post. No stacked text, no starbursts, no price callouts on hero/lifestyle posts.
- **Yellow accent as a whisper.** Logo stays top-left; otherwise use yellow sparingly (a thin rule, one keyword) so it punctuates rather than shouts.
- **`studio` goes cinematic.** Pure black, single rim light, reflection floor — "car as jewellery," reserved for desirable models (Fortuner, Everest, Ranger).
- **`spec_card` stays clean.** Bottom 35% panel limited to 3-4 specs in a quiet hierarchy — our "feature" post, but premium, no bullet soup.
- **`tip_card` = the brand's "helpful expert" voice.** Macro icon, calm typographic layout, one tip per card. Retained for credibility, saves and shares.
- **De-weight the price-led showcase.** `multi_car` drops to a supporting "fresh arrivals" moment and never opens the week.
- **People sparingly and aspirationally.** Max 2 people, authentic SA mix, *doing* something (coffee at a viewpoint, loading bags) — never staring at camera, never a forecourt handshake.
- **Consistency = premium.** Same grade, logo placement and type family across all six templates so the grid reads as one curated gallery.

- **Seedance 2 Mini** on **kie.ai** (`bytedance/seedance-2-mini`) — image-to-video from real-stock reference stills, so the exact in-stock vehicle stays on screen.

- **Cost:** one Seedance 2 Mini generation per clip on kie credits (the cheaper Mini tier) — minimal. Music optional (add in CapCut).
- **The per-car walkaround pipeline (Cloudflare Stream + ffmpeg 9:16) is untouched** — these lifestyle films are a separate, more creative track.

## 7. Format & Platform Variations — getting more from each idea

How we turn one idea into several posts and reach all three platforms (Facebook · Instagram · TikTok).

- **Vertical (9:16) reels — biggest reach win.** Our brand films are 16:9 (great on Facebook). For **Instagram Reels and TikTok**, a native 9:16 cut roughly triples reach. *Both July reels now have a 9:16 vertical version (native re-render, 720p, branded vertical outro) — see the review link.*
- **Carousels — best organic reach on Instagram right now.** Build from a single car's photos: slide 1 hook → slides 2-4 detail shots (interior, boot, dash) → final slide price/CTA. Also ideal for the Trust angle (one promise per slide).
- **Static → motion (free).** Turn any static spotlight into a 5-sec Ken-Burns clip with our `ffmpeg` pipeline — no Seedance cost — for cheap TikTok/Reels volume.
- **Caption A/B.** Same image, two hooks (emotional vs. price-led) to learn what the audience responds to.
- **Recurring named slots** train the audience to expect content: *Feature Friday*, *Just Arrived*, *Tip Tuesday*, *Sunday Drive*.

**Aspect-ratio cheat sheet:** static feed **4:5** (FB + IG) · reels/stories/TikTok **9:16** · carousels **4:5 or 1:1** · landscape brand films **16:9** (FB feed, IG feed video).
