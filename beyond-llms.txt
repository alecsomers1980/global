# Beyond

> Fine dining on Buitenverwachting Wine Estate in Constantia, Cape Town — a sophisticated restaurant where provenance, seasonality and the origins of flavour are the foundation.

Beyond is a two-star fine-dining restaurant on the historic Buitenverwachting Wine Estate in Constantia, Cape Town. The kitchen honours ingredients native to the Western Cape — sourced from the restaurant's own indigenous garden, foraged from the nearby coast, and supplied by local producers — drawing on ancient ingredients, age-old traditions and a deep connection to place and flavour. The dining room looks out over the rolling vineyards through floor-to-ceiling windows, with the warmth of a fireplace lending an elegant yet relaxed atmosphere.

## The kitchen

The kitchen is led by Executive Chef Sebastian Stehr, with guidance from FYN Group's chefs Peter Tempelhoff and Ashley Moss. The menu is ingredient-led and seasonal, celebrating the very best local produce of the Western Cape.

## The beverage programme

Wines from Buitenverwachting express the character of the estate's soils and vineyards. Cocktail and non-alcoholic pairings incorporate indigenous ingredients alongside garden-grown herbs, house-made infusions, fermentations and tinctures. The programme is led by FYN Group Sommelier and Beverage Director Jennifer Hugé, recipient of the Eat Out Guide Patron Mixology Award.
