# Past Productions — Maynardville Open Air Theatre
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/about-us/past-productions

## Captivating Audiences Since 1956
Maynardville Open Air Theatre is nestled within Maynardville Park in Wynberg, Cape Town — a unique 600-seater wooded theatre.

### Heritage / Founders
In the 1950s, **Dulcie Howes, Cecilia Sonnenberg, and Rene Ahrenson** envisioned Maynardville Park as an open-air theatre for an annual Shakespeare festival. They convinced the city to create a permanent stage and auditorium.

Inaugural production: **1956 — "The Taming of the Shrew"**. Renowned actor/director **Leslie French** brought it to life; packed houses and a month-long run established Maynardville as a premier destination for Shakespeare in the Park.
