# Parking — Maynardville
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/parking

## Maynardville Secure Parking
Located at the **Simon Van Der Stel Primary School Sports Field**, just past the Engen Chelsea Village Convenience Centre at **6B Carr Hill Road**.

- **Close by:** 150m from the entrance, security patrolling walkways
- **Affordable:** R50 per vehicle, payable by card on arrival
- **Secure:** Run and staffed by Festival security and personnel
- **Hours:** Opens 2 hours prior to performance until the Food Market bar closes
- **Signage:** Follow the "MV Secure Parking" signs
- **Community benefit:** Proceeds benefit Simon Van Der Stel Primary School and fund growth initiatives at Maynardville Festival

**Free Unsecured Parking** also available by the Wynberg Public Library on Glaren Road or along public roads near the venue.
