# Food Market — Maynardville
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/food-market

## Evening Food Market
Before the show, wander the Maynardville Food Market — artisanal street food and local flavours. Every stall handpicked. Now in its **fourth year**, running for **over 40 nights**.

### The Market Bar
On- and off-consumption options, partnerships and bar takeovers. Opens **2 hours prior to performance until late**.
