# Twelfth Night 2026 (Production)
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/project/twelfth-night-2026

**By** William Shakespeare · **Directed by** Steven Stead
**31 January – 8 March 2026 | Maynardville Theatre**
Tagline: A joyous comedy. A timeless tale of love, deception & mistaken identity.
Tickets (Quicket): https://www.quicket.co.za/events/301564-maynardville-2026-shakespeares-twelfth-night/

- **Running time:** ~100 minutes, no interval
- **Suitability:** All Ages
- **Concept:** Set to a sultry jazz soundtrack echoing 1960s Rome; couture costumes (Sofia Loren-style); inspired by Fellini's *La Dolce Vita*.

## Director's Note (Steven Stead) — summary
Twelfth Night's humour comes from the discomfort of characters wrestling with who they think they are vs who they really are. Set in a Fellini-esque 1960s Italy — sunshine, glamour, theatricality — inspired by *La Dolce Vita* and films of Sophia Loren and Gina Lollobrigida.
