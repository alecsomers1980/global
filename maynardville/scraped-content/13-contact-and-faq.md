## FAQ (key policies for a back-end system)
- **Booking:** Online via Quicket. Box Office on-site opens 2 hours before each performance; tickets available at the gate but advance purchase recommended.
- **Group discounts:** 10–19 → 10% off (auto at Quicket checkout); 20+ → contact boxoffice@ for greater discounts.
- **Disability access:** Ramps and designated wheelchair spaces; contact boxoffice@ for special requests.
- **Children policy:** No under-5s; every attendee needs own ticket & seat (no babes in arms); children in aisle seats where possible; parental discretion for quiet/attentive shows.
- **Picnics:** Allowed (picnic in the Park, not on Food Market seating). No alcohol may be brought in by law; sold at licensed Market bars.
- **Parking:** Front gates at corner of Church and Wolf Street. Secure parking at Simon Van Der Stel Primary (Carr Hill Road); free unsecured parking by Wynberg Public Library (Glaren Road).
- **Refunds/transfers:** Tickets non-refundable; transferable to another date if performance cancelled. Reselling is illegal in SA.
- **Food Market/Bar hours:** Food Market opens 17:30; bar open before and after show.
- **Weather:** Contingency plan per performance. Bring rain jackets/ponchos (no umbrellas). Final call ~30 min before start (rain cancellation call 1 hr before). Updates via Facebook & Instagram. Box Office handles reallocation/refunds.
- **Pets:** Not allowed except service animals.
- **Photography/recording:** Not allowed during performance; OK before/after/intermission.
- **Programmes:** Printed programmes at festival; schedule on website/social.
