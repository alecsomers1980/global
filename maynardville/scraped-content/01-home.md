## Cape Town's iconic celebration under the stars
### Discover the Maynardville Open-Air Festival

Set in the heart of Maynardville Park, our annual summer season blends Shakespearean theatre, ballet, opera, and jazz into one unforgettable cultural experience. As one of the most enchanting Cape Town festivals, Maynardville is where shows in Cape Town become extraordinary nights out.

From world-class performances to a vibrant food market, Maynardville is more than a night of entertainment – it's one of the top Cape Town tourist attractions, a place where memories are made beneath the trees.

Join us at the Maynardville Open-Air Festival – timeless, atmospheric, and unlike anything else in the city.
