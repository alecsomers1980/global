# Sponsors & Partners — Maynardville
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/sponsors-and-partners

## Governance & Producing
### The Maynardville Theatre Trust
Established **1999** by Cecilia Sonnenburg. Guides the future development of the open-air venue in Wynberg. Independent trustees: **Pieter Toerien, David Sonnenberg, Africa Melane, Alan Committie, Lauren Bates, Brian Heydenrych.**

### VR Theatrical
Appointed **producer in 2023**. Award-winning company presenting Broadway plays/musicals and developing original work. https://www.vrtheatrical.com/

### Open Air Collective
Curated food vendor caravan — runs the Maynardville Evening Food Market. https://openaircollective.co.za/

### Open Culture Foundation
Runs the Professional Arts Development Programme (technical theatre training). https://openculturefoundation.org/
