## Discover the Maynardville Festival Programme
Each summer, the historic Maynardville Park transforms into a cultural landmark, hosting a programme of unforgettable performances. From Shakespearean classics to ballet, opera, and jazz, the Maynardville Festival offers a world-class mix of artistry beneath the trees.

Whether you're drawn to the poetry of Shakespeare, the rhythm of a Cape Town jazz festival, or the thrill of live performance, Maynardville brings together the very best of concerts Cape Town presents. With its blend of timeless tradition and bold innovation, the Festival is as much a celebration of summer as it is of the arts.

Join us for evenings where music, theatre, and dance converge with our Food Market.
