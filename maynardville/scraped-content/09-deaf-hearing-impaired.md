# Deaf & Hearing Impaired Audiences — Maynardville
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/deaf-hearing-impaired-audiences

## Inclusive Shakespeare for Deaf and Hearing Impaired Audiences
Following the inaugural accessible performance in 2025, the programme expands in 2026 with a second show (exclusively for adult audiences). **Supported by the National Arts Council.**

### SASL-interpreted performances (2026)
- **Thursday, 5 February 2026** — dedicated performance for school groups (scholars only)
- **Saturday, 7 February 2026** — open to the public

### Commitment / Offers
- Sponsored (free) group tickets for schools, for Deaf scholars under 18
- Discounted tickets at 50% off for Deaf patrons and friends/family attending with them
- Loop system available throughout the complete festival
