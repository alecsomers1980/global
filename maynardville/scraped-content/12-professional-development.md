# Professional Development — Maynardville
Source: https://maynardville.co.za/professional-development

## Professional Arts Development Programme
Maynardville Theatre, in partnership with **Open Culture Foundation** and **Eastern Acoustics**. Now in its **fourth consecutive year** (2026 = 70 years of the Festival). Bridges institutional training and workplace experience via hands-on technical theatre training.

### 2026 expansion
Formal partnership with **Eastern Acoustics** (technical supplier for past 4 years). On-site mentorship covering rigging, plotting, troubleshooting. Time at the Eastern Acoustics warehouse: equipment prep/packing, power & signal distribution, handling of pro lighting/sound/rigging equipment.

### Programme scope
Participants deliver the full technical infrastructure across Music Concerts, Opera, Ballet, and the annual Shakespeare production:
- Stage management & rehearsal support
- Lighting & sound setup/operation
- Wardrobe, set, props maintenance
- Front-of-house & backstage duties

Seeking **three (3)** graduates.
