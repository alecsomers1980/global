### Attribution obligations

Licences in use: `CC BY 2.0`, `CC BY 3.0`, `CC BY-SA 4.0`, `CC0`, `Public domain`.

- **CC BY** and **CC BY-SA** require visible credit to the photographer.
- **CC BY-SA** additionally applies share-alike terms to derivative works of the image.
- **Public domain / CC0** need no credit, but are listed here for traceability.

These credits must remain reachable from the live site. Replacing stock with the
client's own photography removes the obligation entirely, which is the preferred outcome.
