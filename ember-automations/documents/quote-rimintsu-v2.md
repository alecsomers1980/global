## The Problem: How Things Work Now

| Challenge | Current WordPress Reality |
|---|---|
| **Article publishing** | Manually written, formatted, and uploaded by staff |
| **SEO** | Dependent on staff knowledge and plugins — inconsistent |
| **Ad management** | Ads placed and removed manually — no tier system |
| **Client reporting** | No automated reporting — ad clients have zero visibility |
| **Social media** | Manually posting to each platform separately, or not at all |
| **Newsletter** | Manual design and send — time-consuming, often skipped |
