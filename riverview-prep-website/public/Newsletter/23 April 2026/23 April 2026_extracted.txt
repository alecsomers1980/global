Senior Primary Camp at Three Sisters, Lows Creek  
The Senior Primary children had a truly memorable experience at their annual camp this year. 
Three Sisters Camp, newly constructed and set in breathtaking natural surroundings, provided the perfect backdrop 
for a week of learning, growth, and fun. 
The ANTs facilitators were outstanding, offering a wide range of relevant and engaging activities. The children were 
divided into teams and took part in experiences such as mapwork and orienteering, hands-on learning about biodi-
versity and food chains, obstacle courses, archery, and spear throwing. These activities not only challenged them 
physically but also encouraged problem-solving and collaboration. 
Evenings were just as special, with opportunities to showcase their talents in a lively talent show and to sit around 
the campfire under the starry night sky. These moments created lasting memories and strengthened friendships. 
A key focus of the camp was developing teamwork and helping each child recognise the valuable role they play 
within a group. Alongside the physical activities, quieter, reflective sessions allowed the children to engage in 
meaningful discussions about their identity and the hope that we all have in Jesus.

Some of the children shared what they learned from camp: 
“Making friends, learning to be independent, realising that I can be a leader, responsibility and leadership, looking

All in all, it was a wonderful and enriching camp experience. Thank you to the staff who generously gave their time 
to accompany and guide the children, and to the parents for giving them the opportunity to learn and grow in such 
a beautiful environment.

Pre–School News 
April showers bring curious minds! Our preschoolers didn’t let the clouds stop the learning, playing or the smiles. 
Writing numbers builds fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and early number sense. It 
helps children connect symbols to quantity, understand order, and recognize numbers in 
everyday life. This early practise lays the groundwork for confidence in Mathematics and 
school readiness. 
This week, the Cubs had lots of fun learning through play! Play is an important part of their day because it helps 
them learn, grow, and build confidence. 
Outdoor play gave them time to run, jump, climb, and make their bodies stronger, this gave them an idea on how 
to share, take turns, and play nicely with their friends.
