**On the AI / "overseas" concern**

- Sending information to AI abroad isn't actually banned under POPIA — it's allowed when there's a proper data-protection agreement in place, or with the person's consent. So it's a contract-and-consent matter, not a dead end.
- But we'd go further than the minimum: the AI never needs to see a full CV. We keep the sensitive, identifying details — names, ID numbers, and anything about race, health or disability — **inside South Africa**, and only ever send AI a **de-identified version**, so it works on skills and experience, not on who the person is.
- Anything specially protected under POPIA (race, health, disability) is **stripped out before AI is involved at all**.
- Where AI helps with shortlisting later, **a person always makes the final call** — never the machine on its own.

The result is a system that does everything you need, built privacy-first — which actually puts you **ahead** of agencies handling this carelessly on shared platforms. It becomes something you can sell to your clients, not apologise for.
