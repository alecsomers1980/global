## 3. What we changed, and why it matters

### 3.1 A modern, on-brand design
**What:** A clean, professional layout with a looping water-video banner, your logo, your blue-and-earth colour scheme and the Poppins font throughout.
**Why it matters:** First impressions decide whether a visitor trusts you enough to call. A modern site signals a modern, capable company — exactly what someone spending tens of thousands of rand on a borehole wants to see.

### 3.2 Your services, properly explained (6 dedicated pages)
**What:** We expanded from 3 services to the **6 you actually offer** — Borehole Drilling, Pump Installation, Water Filtration & Storage, Water Analysis & Testing, Water Treatment & Chemicals, and Off-Grid Solar Solutions. Each has its own page with clear, professionally written content.
**Why it matters:** Customers (and Google) need to know the full range of what you do. Each service page can rank separately on Google for that specific search (e.g. "solar borehole pump Johannesburg"), bringing in enquiries the old site never could.

### 3.3 Easy to contact — including WhatsApp
**What:** A floating WhatsApp button on every page (one tap to chat), plus a "request a quote" form that emails the enquiry straight to you, and click-to-call phone links.
**Why it matters:** In South Africa, WhatsApp is how most people prefer to make first contact. Removing friction here directly increases the number of enquiries you receive.

### 3.4 Genuinely fast — especially on mobile
**What:** We optimised every image, used an efficient video, and built the site so the important content appears almost instantly. We measured it with Google's own tool (Lighthouse) — see Section 4.
**Why it matters:** More than half of visitors leave a site that takes more than ~3 seconds to load, and Google ranks faster sites higher. Speed is both a customer-experience and a Google-ranking win.

### 3.5 Built mobile-first
**What:** Most people will visit from a phone. Menus, tap targets, images and the layout are all designed for small screens first.
**Why it matters:** A site that's awkward on a phone loses the majority of its visitors. This one is effortless on mobile.

### 3.6 More secure
**What:** The new site has **no WordPress and no database** to hack, and we added professional security protections (the kind of "security headers" banks use). The quote form is protected against spam and abuse.
**Why it matters:** WordPress sites are the most-hacked websites in the world. Removing it eliminates that whole category of risk — no more worrying about plug-in updates and break-ins.

### 3.7 Found on Google — and AI search
**What:** We added the technical foundations search engines look for: a sitemap, "structured data" (which lets Google show rich results), local-area targeting for your Gauteng suburbs, and we explicitly **allowed AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google's AI) to read and cite the site.**
**Why it matters:** People increasingly find services through Google *and* through AI assistants. Most competitors haven't set up for AI search yet — doing it now puts East Lake ahead.

## 7. Recommendations — how to stand out and win more work

Prioritised so you can pick what to do first. We can implement any of these.

### Quick wins (high impact, low effort)
1. **Set up & link a Google Business Profile** and start collecting **Google reviews**, then show the star rating on the site. *This is the single biggest lever for local trust and for being recommended by AI tools.*
2. **Add real testimonials / client logos** (estates, businesses, well-known clients you've drilled for).
3. **Add trust numbers** — e.g. "X years in business", "X boreholes drilled", warranty terms — shown prominently. (We've used only facts so far; give us the real figures and we'll feature them.)
4. **A proper share image** for when the site is sent on WhatsApp/Facebook (currently uses the logo).
5. **Connect the quote form** to email (needs a Resend key) and add a **spam honeypot/CAPTCHA**.

### Medium effort (strong SEO & lead value)
6. **"What does a borehole cost?" guide page** — this is one of the most-searched questions in the industry and a magnet for both Google and AI search. Honest ranges + what affects price.
7. **Certificate of Compliance (COC) & borehole registration** info/service page — many Gauteng property sales now require this; very few competitors explain it well. Big differentiator.
8. **Per-suburb landing pages** (e.g. "Borehole drilling in Fourways/Sandton/Midrand") to capture local searches.
9. **A short blog / guides section** (water-shedding, maintenance tips, solar payback). Fresh content keeps you ranking and feeds AI citations.
10. **Live chat / WhatsApp Business** with quick replies.

### Bigger bets (differentiation)
11. **Before/after project gallery with short captions** ("60 m borehole, Bryanston, 2,000 L/hr") — proof of work that competitors rarely show well.
12. **Short video content** (a drilling-day reel, a customer story) — huge in this space on TikTok/Instagram/YouTube, and embeddable on the site.
13. **An interactive "borehole cost estimator"** — answer a few questions, get an indicative range, capture the lead. Almost no competitor has this.
14. **Financing / payment-plan messaging**, if you can offer it — removes the biggest objection to a large purchase.
